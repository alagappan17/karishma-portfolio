## Overview

A large-scale manufacturing platform connecting production data, AI workflows, quality analytics, and digital twins across global factories.

## The challenge

- Production, quality, and maintenance data had complex relationships.
- AI workflows were difficult for non-technical users to understand.
- The platform needed accessibility and scalability across many modules and locations.

## Design goals

- Improve usability across advanced manufacturing modules.
- Connect sequencing, quality, production, and AI configuration workflows.
- Establish a scalable, accessible design system.

## My role

- Facilitated co-design workshops with engineers, managers, and data scientists.
- Defined information architecture and high-fidelity enterprise workflows.
- Built atomic components and supported delivery, QA, and adoption.
