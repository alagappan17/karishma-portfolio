## Overview

Zora AI is an enterprise digital workforce for sourcing and procurement, pairing autonomous execution with decision-grade intelligence and human-readable recommendations.

## The challenge

- Procurement teams manage high-volume transactions across fragmented systems.
- Leaders need timely insight into spend, sourcing, suppliers, and contracts.
- Automation must preserve governance and human oversight.

## Design goals

- Reduce manual effort while keeping people in control.
- Make recommendations understandable and actionable in workflow.
- Connect strategic insight with transactional execution.

## The workforce model

- Perform workers execute repeatable procurement tasks.
- Advise workers analyze information, identify trends, and recommend action.
- Together they connect insight to execution across procurement work.

## Key screens and features

- CPO Insights for performance, risk, savings, and opportunity.
- Advisors for category management, intake, sourcing, and contracts.
- Autonomous purchase order creation with validation and approval checkpoints.

## Key learnings

Enterprise AI needs visible reasoning, clear handoffs, and meaningful review points. Autonomy earns trust when people can understand, validate, and override it.
