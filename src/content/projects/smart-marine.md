## Overview

Smart Marine unified container tracking, purchase orders, inventory planning, and shipment documents in a single partner portal for logistics and procurement teams.

## The challenge

- Shipment information and documents lived across fragmented tools.
- Teams struggled to connect containers, purchase orders, and seasonal planning.
- Large data sets needed to stay usable without becoming overwhelming.

## Design goals

- Create one source of truth for shipment and purchase order visibility.
- Make documents searchable, connected, and easier to manage.
- Surface delays, missing paperwork, and important arrivals early.

## My role

- Ran requirements workshops and interviewed supply chain specialists.
- Mapped logistics, procurement, and documentation workflows.
- Designed wireframes, prototypes, specifications, and responsive behavior.

## Key screens and features

![Shipment tracking workspace](placeholder:tracking)

- Purchase order management with linked shipment status.
- Live container tracking with location, ETA, vessel, and delay details.
- Event and inventory planning views for seasonal milestones.
- Document library with guided upload and metadata review.

## Testing and iteration

Testing led to collapsible filters, clearer inventory views, and metadata auto-fill patterns for document uploads.

## Key learnings

Complex operational products need progressive disclosure and strong relationships between data, documents, and next actions.
