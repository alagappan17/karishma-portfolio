## Overview

An AI contract platform that combined conversational search, document ingestion, and human-in-the-loop review for complex healthcare and life-sciences agreements.

## The challenge

- Teams needed to process, connect, and validate large sets of complex documents.
- Contract information was difficult to search, compare, and trace.
- AI-parsed content needed trustworthy review and approval controls.

## Design goals

- Make contract information easier to find and understand.
- Preserve human control over parsing, validation, and approval.
- Show relationships between base agreements, amendments, and related documents.

## My role

Designed conversational search, ingestion and review workflows, content validation, sequencing, library views, and living-document patterns.

## Key screens and features

- Natural-language search with source-linked answers.
- Ingestion queues with confidence indicators and review actions.
- Side-by-side original and parsed content validation.
- Document sequencing, relationship mapping, and version-aware library views.

## Key learnings

Trustworthy document AI needs traceability, meaningful confidence signals, and human validation embedded throughout the lifecycle.
