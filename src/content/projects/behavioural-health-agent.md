## Overview

An AI-powered conversational experience designed to help people in emotional distress find calm, low-effort guidance and reach verified human support. It was designed as an initial support layer, not a replacement for clinical or emergency care.

## The challenge

- People in distress can struggle to explain what they need or process complex choices.
- Traditional support services can feel difficult to approach.
- The experience required clear safety, fallback, and escalation pathways.

## Design goals

- Reduce cognitive effort and provide practical next steps.
- Use calm, non-judgmental language and user-controlled pacing.
- Make professional and emergency support easy to find.

## My role

- Mapped behavioral health journeys and discussed needs with therapists and case managers.
- Designed conversational flows, fallback states, and escalation paths.
- Created prototypes for usability and scenario testing.

## Key screens and features

- Welcome states for talking, breathing support, or therapist discovery.
- Crisis conversation with free text and simple response options.
- Therapist matching with verified support and escalation choices.
- Safety monitoring views focused on service improvement, not clinical evaluation.

## Key learnings

Empathy must show up in language, pacing, layout, and control. The least-effort path matters most when a person is overwhelmed.
