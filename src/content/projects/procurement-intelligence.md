## Overview

A connected suite of procurement dashboards that gave teams a clearer view of contracts, suppliers, materials, sourcing, spend, and operational risk.

## The challenge

- Teams needed to understand supplier performance and procurement risk across regions.
- Large datasets made it easy to lose context.
- Multiple personas needed different priorities from a shared data foundation.

## Design goals

- Make risks, deadlines, and exceptions visible earlier.
- Support movement from portfolio signals to detailed records.
- Establish a shared pattern library for interconnected dashboard experiences.

## My role

- Structured information architecture across contracts, suppliers, materials, and spend.
- Designed dashboard layouts, data tables, filters, status patterns, and drill-down behavior.
- Created persona-based views for portfolio and supplier management.

## Key screens and features

- Contract lifecycle and exemption monitoring.
- Spend and savings analysis with comparison views.
- Supplier grouping, risk, performance, and onboarding views.
- Materials, sourcing, and RFX milestone tracking.

## Key learnings

Data becomes useful when it is organized around decisions and actions. Charts reveal patterns while tables support investigation.
