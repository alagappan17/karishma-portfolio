## Overview

A centralized hub of specialized AI agents that helps marketing teams plan campaigns, understand performance, and receive useful insight through familiar collaboration tools.

## The challenge

- Campaign teams worked across briefs, reports, collaboration tools, and dashboards.
- New AI capabilities needed to feel understandable and task-oriented.
- Notification preferences had to respect individual workflows.

## Design goals

- Help people discover the right AI support for each job.
- Turn performance information into concise, actionable guidance.
- Create a consistent foundation for future agents.

## My role

- Designed the hub, agent discovery patterns, and onboarding.
- Created campaign insight, notification, and collaboration experiences.
- Defined empty and first-use states for evolving AI capabilities.

## Key screens and features

- Digital Brief Agent for structured campaign inputs.
- Campaign Insight Agent with trends, warnings, and next actions.
- Ask Orchestrator that routes people to relevant support.
- Notification setup for schedule, time zone, frequency, and channel.

## Key learnings

AI should explain its value in the language of the task. Insights become useful when they arrive with a clear next step.
