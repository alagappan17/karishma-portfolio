## Overview

A finance operations workspace that replaced spreadsheet and email coordination with shared visibility into monthly and quarterly close activities.

## The challenge

- Teams lacked a central view of progress, ownership, and risk.
- Closing methods varied across business units.
- Managers learned about risks too late to intervene.

## Design goals

- Standardize close activities while allowing appropriate variation.
- Make deadlines, progress, and escalation paths clear.
- Reduce cognitive load during high-pressure work.

## My role

- Mapped workflows with product and finance specialists.
- Turned bottlenecks into wireframes, a clickable prototype, and final designs.
- Validated concepts through stakeholder reviews.

## Key screens and features

![Financial close workspace](placeholder:close)

- Close calendar and entity progress tracking.
- Task detail with ownership, status, due date, and escalation actions.
- Reusable checklist templates for recurring close activities.

## Key learnings

The most useful enterprise views answer four questions quickly: what is due, what is at risk, who owns it, and what is complete.
