## Overview

AI Assist embedded generative AI into Jira workflows for product owners, architects, QA specialists, UI designers, and administrators while keeping people in control of what gets published.

## The challenge

- Requirements and test documentation took significant manual effort.
- Teams needed AI output to remain reviewable, editable, and traceable.
- Each role needed relevant support inside a shared workflow.

## Design goals

- Improve the quality and completeness of delivery artifacts.
- Support role-specific assistance without fragmenting the product.
- Maintain human review, clear publishing, and traceable handoffs.

## My role

- Led end-to-end UX strategy and mentored a team of three designers.
- Mapped journeys and decision trees across assist modules.
- Established the design system and designed the Admin Portal.

- A five-step framework from context to review and publishing.

## Key learnings

AI experiences work best when user control, traceability, and modular assistance are designed into the workflow from the start.
