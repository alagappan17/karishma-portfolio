# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Recruiters and hiring managers evaluating Karishma Sharma for senior product-design roles. They need a quick, credible sense of her craft, seniority, product thinking, and relevant enterprise AI experience before deciding whether to review a case study or begin a conversation.

## Product Purpose

Karishma Sharma’s personal product-design portfolio introduces her perspective and selected body of work. The homepage is the concise front door; selected projects will later expand into long-form case-study pages.

## Positioning

Karishma is a senior product designer who makes complex enterprise and AI systems clear, trustworthy, and usable. Her practice pairs hands-on interface craft with UX strategy for high-stakes, human-in-the-loop workflows.

## Operating Context

The portfolio is shared during senior-design hiring processes. Visitors first scan an editorial homepage, then move to selected case studies for project depth, and can use a Calendly invitation to start a conversation.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The current scope is the homepage skeleton; the application must be ready to grow into individual case-study pages.
- All display content must live in structured JSON files in a single `src/data` folder, not directly in React components.
- Use placeholder visuals for project work until approved Figma exports are available.
- Keep animation minimal in this iteration.
- The Calendly URL is a dummy placeholder for now.

## Brand Commitments

- Personal, modern, minimal, elegant, and visually eye-catching.
- A bento-box composition with rounded corners and intentionally varied tile proportions.
- A rotating family of subtle light color fields—warm parchment, powder blue, pale butter, and muted lilac—with dark ink contrast.
- Typography-led introduction with a modern sans-serif and expressive display face.

## Evidence on Hand

- `docs/Karishma_Resume.docx`: professional experience, skills, recognition, and contact details.
- `docs/portfolio-case-studies.docx`: twelve website-ready project narratives and selected-work recommendations.
- The featured homepage projects are Zora AI, AI-Powered Contracts Navigator, and Smart Marine.
- Project details and naming are draft content and can change later; use the supplied names and descriptions for this iteration.

## Product Principles

1. Make senior-level craft and judgment apparent before detail.
2. Let complex work look calm, legible, and considered.
3. Favor a concise, personal introduction over an exhaustive homepage index.
4. Build content and page structure for easy iteration and extension.
5. Make the next conversation easy to initiate without turning the portfolio into a sales funnel.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic HTML, keyboard-accessible controls, visible focus states, sufficient contrast, and motion that respects reduced-motion preferences.
